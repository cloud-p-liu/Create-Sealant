# Sealant maintenance rules

Read [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) and [chamber architecture](docs/architecture/chambers.md) first. This file contains active rules. Superseded approaches belong in [history](docs/history/2026-10-30-development-log.md).

## Simulation contracts

- Preserve three SERVER world modes: default LIGHTWEIGHT (sealed displacement buoyancy, no leakage), FIELD_LOW (5 Hz), and FIELD_FULL (20 Hz). Lightweight mode is independent of Field V3: pause and retain cell state, never flatten, clear, or convert it into rooms. Resume it on return to field mode.
- A field chamber is a controllable 3D computation domain. Preserve each cell's reference water volume W, GasPV, local pressure, and each open face's flux. Never restore room/connected-region average water levels or pressure. Connectivity may accelerate solving but must not remove cell degrees of freedom.
- Same-owner chambers join through positive-volume overlap or positive-area coplanar contact. Deduplicate union volume and internal faces; different owners do not group. Geometric grouping does not imply uniform physical state.
- Shared-face exchange must conserve both phases and retain bounded, positive implicit solving. Internal countercurrent displacement must not apply to external reservoirs: preserve diving-bell trapped air. Do not conceal problems with tiny-water clearing, global averaging, or reduced buoyancy.
- Preserve V3 spatial parcels and V2/V1 migration. New obstruction parks mass at its original location; do not distribute it through walls. Reference water volume differs from compressed visible volume. Face velocities are not persisted.
- Declared block occupancy follows approved scheme A: default 1.0, air/liquid/kelp/kelp_plant 0, slab/stair exceptions and SERVER state rules. Snow layers occupy 0; soul sand and honey blocks occupy 1. Capacity comes from declared values, never internal voxel/volume samples. LOW uses 2 × 2 and FULL 4 × 4 face panels solely for passages. Analytic shape subtraction locates V3 parcels/heights and must normalize to declared capacity. See [block volumes](docs/architecture/block-volumes.md).
- Keep declared-volume intersections and face geometry lazy: rebuild only for effective shape/block/rule/pose changes. Cache continuous external root-world water-column tops and evaluate head at the current world sample Y. Invalidate on fluid/neighbor/chunk changes and never bridge dry gaps. Internal fields remain local cells, not water columns or rooms. Do not cache fixed pressure forces. See [lazy geometry](docs/architecture/lazy-geometry.md).
- Closed groups may sleep exchange only after per-face rest checks of wet head, shared dry gas pressure, existing transport, and countercurrent displacement. Do not eliminate hydrostatic gradients by demanding equal base pressures. Whole-group absolute residual tolerance is 0.000400 block³/game tick, without cancellation. Old dry-donor water momentum is not transportable water. Report water/gas/displacement separately; do not keep raising tolerance for unexplained peaks. Sleep skips exchange only, retains forces/EOS/W/G/flux, and inherits only consecutive-rest observations. Recheck current openings and physical differences every update. Collision-body count, geometry identity, or pose change alone must not prevent sleep; no carrier-only exception. Actual compression/head/path changes wake solving in the same update. Runtime sleep is not persisted. See [sleep](docs/architecture/fluid-sleep.md).

## Selection and coordinates

- Preserve the verified upstream honey-glue two-corner selection, Level.clip, Sable plot coordinates, and Outliner workflow. A local immutable baseline may exist at `reference/verified-honey-glue-2026-10-29/`; do not overwrite it or require it to build. Preserve upstream MIT attribution.
- Keep `sealant:chamber_structure` in BOTH `retain_in_sub_level.json` and `destroy_with_sub_level.json`. Missing retention moves markers out of the plot and breaks rotated alignment.
- Local chamber dimensions are fixed after creation. Never overwrite them with a world AABB, add resize packets, or implement Ctrl-scroll resizing. Convert inclusive maximum block indices to outer edges with +1. Outliner receives local boxes, projected once by Sable renderPose.
- Actual OBB and overlap outlines use #59B5E8. F3+B works without a held item. SAT is contact detection only: no displacement vector/MTV forces. Exclude the carrier from other-body overlap outlines. Use “chamber” in English and “氣室” in Traditional Chinese localization.

## Native gates and rigid-body forces

- Manual native buoyancy/drag user_data and automatic transient takeover are separate. Effective disable = manual OR any chamber takeover. Exit/deletion/auto-off restores manual intent; never overwrite manual flags.
- Default entry requires contact with the original OBB inset by 0.125 blocks; retained takeover uses original-OBB contact until exit. The inset affects only new entry, not local dimensions, breathing, or camera dryness. Auto-off, deletion, and unload clear transient state.
- Optional SERVER `takeover.intersectionVolume` defaults false. When enabled, only other Sable bodies use the deduplicated connected same-owner union for complete entry/exit, replacing the inset. Fixed internal 99% entry / 1% exit comparisons supply approximately 1% boundary tolerance; intermediate states retain prior takeover. Do not expose ratio settings or percentage reports. Carrier, vanilla entities, breathing, and camera rules stay independent. Volumes/centroids are diagnostic and gating data, never MTV or proportional forces. Disabled means no clipping calculation.
- The carrier is managed. Other structures use the selected OBB rule, never center-point or world enclosing-AABB tests. Vanilla entities/boats retain external-water suppression on contact. FIELD Player swimming uses actual wet cells and exact domain-exterior water clipping; logical eye points drive breathing. No CLIENT display option or render interpolation may determine player physics. Other mobs/boats lack virtual internal swimming; finite oxygen is not implemented. See [player water](docs/architecture/player-swimming.md).
- Native buoyancy changes only a specified plot's collider displacement volume through a separate bakery/cache, never global BlockState colliders, mass, or collision shape. Sable loads the plot before user_data; verify the effective mode before native prePhysicsTicks.
- Drag gating wraps Rapier3D.tick force collection only, never native step. Restore every masked velocity in finally, including exceptions.
- Noncarrier field bodies integrate local pressure on their own collision surface, without duplicate grid-face loads. Uniform gas pressure must give zero net force/torque. Retain low-frequency hydrostatic integration, but solve noncarrier EOS implicitly from current pose/capacity each physics substep. Do not restore LOW's held gas force. Never pair stale world normals with newly rotated points. Shape caches invalidate on actual shape changes/section additions, not metadata buoyancy refreshes. See [pressure coupling](docs/architecture/pressure-coupling.md).
- Synthetic energy checks do not establish gameplay correctness. The user confirmed that the specific LOW dry-cylinder 100-block ejection disappeared and rebound became acceptable. Do not extrapolate to every mass, rotation, or boundary. Excessive backpressure/grid trapping remain unresolved.
- Lightweight displacement uses the deduplicated chamber union and buoyancy centroids that follow OBB tilt; never inflate volume using a world AABB. Preserve 6DOF drag. Unknown external-water chunks suspend the whole force evaluation instead of guessing partial forces.
- JNI/pipeline impulses are local: transform world force/torque and multiply by each actual physics-substep dt. Field gauge pressure acts inward from exterior boundaries and outward from neighboring internal cells. Never add the old solid-volume-gradient force. Preserve the implicit 6DOF water-drag energy regression.

## Rendering, configuration, and tools

- Render with renderPose and shared-vertex marching tetrahedra. Visual interpolation must never write into physics. Subtract solids from sloping interfaces with 3D polygons, not horizontal slices.
- External sea culling uses CLIENT shader water-atlas UVs and camera-relative renderPose OBBs. Do not clip glass, modify water blocks, or register Sable WaterOcclusionContainer. Current adapter targets vanilla/Sable VANILLA; Sodium/Iris are unsupported. FIELD culling has separate validation limits.
- Lightweight breathing maps only WATER to AIR at the exact logical eye point. Do not use body AABBs, takeover insets, local display switches, canceled damage, or directly refilled air. SERVER mode/inset sync controls physics.
- Water surfaces/overlays use vanilla atlas animation and biome colors; clip UVs to unit tiles before atlas mapping. Inner water overlays belong on actual inner glass faces, exterior water on exterior faces. Do not project through walls or use the translucent pass clipped by the external sea mask.
- Camera/water-particle dryness uses renderPose and the same visible tetrahedral surface at a point. Player physics uses raw logical cells and poses. No shared feedback, body-AABB camera test, or physics-affecting water occlusion container. Filter water particles only, retaining smoke/fire.
- Internal structures inherit parent-group average SKY/BLOCK lighting while retaining stronger own sources. The outermost layer retains native sky attenuation. Do not restore water-column-top lighting or fullbright. Update both ends of dual-channel shader encoding together.
- FIELD pink air debug represents actual GasPV/P sampling, never vacuum. Preserve water-above-air exchange, fully flooded fronts, diving-bell, and crack-free shared faces. Lightweight pink points are explicitly assumed-dry samples.
- Persist local preferences through SealantClientConfig and reuse Create/Catnip BaseConfigScreen. World physics uses SealantServerConfig SERVER spec, permission level 2, and ValueSpec-validated packets; never CLIENT physics. Do not expose unverified constants in UI.
- Inspector world actions are a closed enum, server-checked at permission 2. Reset explicitly confirms the marker UUID and server-rechecks the current target. Never trust client permissions, arbitrary command strings, or arbitrary UUIDs. Display switches do not change server physics.

## Verification and handoff

- If a human is better suited to a test (gameplay feel, visual judgment, acceptable oscillation), proactively request user testing with short steps and observations. Finish all agent-run preparation/checks first. Keep that aspect unverified until the human reports; compilation cannot replace it.
- Build with Java 21: `./gradlew build --offline`. Geometry/resource/fluid/gate regressions run through check. Coordinate/render changes require rotated gameplay retesting; a build is not visual validation.
- Respect existing positive reports for selection, native gates/restoration, auto takeover, parent lighting, field exchange, water continuity, pink gas debug, original UI, lightweight sea culling/breathing/inset entry, and corrected inner overlay placement. New UI or changed physics requires its own validation record.
- Profile queries, collider changes, field updates, and rendering separately. Synthetic benchmarks are not TPS. Recent prototype performance was acceptable to the user, without controlled A/B evidence or a general guarantee.
- Do not claim Sodium/Iris/Flywheel compatibility, thin-gap accuracy, full two-phase CFD, universal rendering support, finite oxygen, or complete FIELD player swimming/breathing validation. The revised complete-entry/exit rule still needs a separate feel test; prior ratio-policy success is not that test. Never claim zero oscillation or all scenarios verified.
- Never force-restart a running world without coordination. Once normal saves and shutdown are evidenced, an already-authorized build may be loaded.
- Update README/HANDOFF/current technical docs after changes. Archive superseded workflows instead of appending contradictory “latest” specifications. Public documentation is English; preserve localized game resources.
